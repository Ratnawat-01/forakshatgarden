About

Know more about Akshat Marriage Garden

  • A Spacious & Elegant Venue

    Akshat Marriage Garden offers a large, open and well-maintained lawn suitable for weddings, receptions and grand family functions. The layout allows comfortable seating and smooth movement for guests.

  • Beautiful Décor Possibilities

    The garden supports a wide range of decoration styles – traditional, modern, floral or theme-based – allowing families to customise the setup according to their function and budget.

  • Essential Amenities Available

    From parking space and lighting to power backup and guest comfort, Akshat Marriage Garden provides all basic amenities required for smooth and hassle-free events.

  • Convenient Location in Indore

    Located in a well-connected area of Indore, the venue is easily accessible for guests, making it a convenient choice for both local and outstation families.
