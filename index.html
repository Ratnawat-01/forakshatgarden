ABOUT AKSHAT GARDEN

Know More About Akshat Marriage Garden

  • Established in 2004

    A Legacy of Grand Celebrations

    With over 20+ years of legacy, Akshat Marriage Garden has been a preferred destination for grand weddings. We have successfully hosted over 3,000+ events, building a reputation for excellence and trust.

  • Prime Location in Indore

    Heart of the City

    Located at a prime spot near Shri Vaishnav Polytechnic College, M.O.G. Lines. Our venue offers easy accessibility for local and outstation guests, ensuring a hassle-free experience.

  • Professional Management

    A Unit of Anjali Creative Constructions Pvt. Ltd.

    Managed by a team of dedicated professionals under the banner of Anjali Creative Constructions Pvt. Ltd. to ensure every detail of your event is executed to perfection.

    51,000 Sq. Ft. of Grandeur

    Spanning over 51,000 sq. ft., our venue provides ample space for large gatherings, grand reception stages, and elaborate decorations without feeling crowded.

  • Trusted by Thousands

    We take pride in having been a part of thousands of happy beginnings. Your trust is our biggest achievement, and we strive to make every moment memorable.
